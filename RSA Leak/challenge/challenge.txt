------------------------------------------------------------
  ANALYST NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------

RSA Security depends on the difficulty of factoring the
modulus 'n' into its two prime factors 'p' and 'q'.

If you can find p and q, you can compute:
  phi(n) = (p - 1) * (q - 1)
  d      = e^(-1) mod phi(n)   (modular inverse)
  m      = c^d mod n           (plaintext as integer)

Then convert 'm' from an integer back to bytes to read
the message.
